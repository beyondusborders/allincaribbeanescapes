Come for the event

What’s happening

Auténtico spreads the table across Aruba. Restaurant Week and limited culinary experiences run October 3–9. The festival closes with the Auténtico Pavilion in downtown Oranjestad on October 10 and 11, from 6:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m.

The Pavilion brings participating chefs, restaurants and producers together in one place. The official ticket page currently lists both Pavilion nights as waitlist only, so check availability before making a nonrefundable travel booking.

Stay all-in

Divi Aruba All Inclusive

A low-rise beachfront base in Oranjestad, with shared access to the neighboring Tamarijn resort and a staffed concierge for the pieces that happen off property.

Why it fits

Divi Aruba sits in the low-rise hotel area, close enough for a straightforward taxi ride into downtown while keeping the beach portion of the getaway genuinely all-inclusive.

What’s included

All-inclusive dining and bars across Divi and Tamarijn, daily activities, nightly entertainment, Wi-Fi, taxes and service charges, plus the resort shuttle between participating properties and resort areas.

Event transportation

The complimentary resort shuttle does not advertise service to Auténtico. Plan on a taxi or rental car for downtown Pavilion nights and for island-wide Restaurant Week venues. Ask the concierge to arrange the trip and return before you go.

Excursion help

The on-property concierge is available daily from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. for island activities and arrangements. Excursions are optional and generally cost extra; confirm operator, pickup point, cancellation terms and accessibility directly.

Getting there

Fly into Aruba, then take the short road

Use Queen Beatrix International Airport (AUA). Aruba Airport’s current route information shows nonstop service from major U.S. gateways including New York, Boston and Miami, with other seasonal or scheduled service varying by travel period.

Miami · MIAA major southern gateway with frequent nonstop service.
New York · JFK/EWRAruba’s largest U.S. gateway market; check the operating airport carefully.
Boston · BOSA useful Northeast nonstop gateway, with schedules varying by season.

Airfare and airport transfer are not included

Divi confirms that standard all-inclusive rates include taxes and service charges, but not airfare or airport transportation. Use a regulated airport taxi or prebook a shared or private De Palm transfer. Always check the exact room offer: certain premium room categories may list transfer benefits separately.
